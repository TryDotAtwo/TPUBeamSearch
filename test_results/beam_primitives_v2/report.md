## Packing only: matched synchronized timings

65536 candidates/device, 524288 aggregate. Identical integer inputs for all four
variants; 3 warmup rounds and 21 alternating forward/reverse synchronized rounds.
Times include the complete eight-device primitive call, not just device compute;
placement and compilation excluded. Integer outputs exact on every replica.

| Variant | Median ms | p10..p90 ms | Aggregate Mcandidates/s | Matched serial/pipeline ratio |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| serial b2 | 1.347320 | 1.328280..1.382170 | 389.134 | — |
| serial b3 | 1.335930 | 1.318040..1.361111 | 392.452 | — |
| pipeline b2 | 0.670960 | 0.633790..0.687720 | 781.400 | 2.008x |
| pipeline b3 | 0.632890 | 0.605360..0.687340 | 828.403 | 2.111x |

This is an observed ~2x improvement for the metadata pack primitive, NOT inference
or whole beam speedup. No profiler trace was collected, so no claim that all DMA
is hidden or that these times establish the complete overlap mechanism. b3's
median is ~6% better than b2 but their sample ranges overlap; no final universal
buffer-count recommendation follows.

Routing is also exact; its diagnostic median is 0.467350 ms (p10 0.436920,
p90 0.484731) for 256 hashes/device. Do not compare this unlike workload's latency
to packing as a speedup or extrapolate it to a production-sized search.

## New compiler rejections after gather fixes

The previous gather-form failures are passed. Hash/goal now fails on scalar
uint8 extraction: `Only 32-bit scalar vector.extracts supported. Cast your input
to a 32-bit type first.` The observed MLIR op extracts i8 from vector<1xi8>.
Dedup now reaches an unsupported `scatter` generated by the constant sentinel
construction `.at[6].set(...)`. Neither category has an execution timing.

V3 candidate fixes: load/cast the central-state vector to uint32 and compare a
gathered vector rather than scalar uint8; build neutral metadata with broadcast
selection rather than scatter. Two structural regressions reproduce the V2
failures before fixes. Real TPU compilation remains a separate gate.
